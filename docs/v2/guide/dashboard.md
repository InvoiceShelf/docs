# Dashboard

The dashboard summarizes the company currently selected in the application. Check the company name in the top bar before interpreting the figures.

## Read the overview

The four summary cards show **Amount Due**, **Customers**, **Invoices** and **Estimates**. Select a card to open the corresponding list.

![InvoiceShelf 2 dashboard with a populated demo company](/images/v2/dashboard.webp)

## Compare activity over time

The **Sales & Expenses** chart shows Sales, Receipts, Expenses and Net Income. Change the year with the chart’s period selector. Hover over a month to inspect its figures.

Invoices and receipts describe different events: creating an invoice does not itself record a payment. Check the [payments](./payments.md) and [expenses](./expenses.md) lists when reconciling a total.

## Follow up on outstanding work

Open an entry under **Due invoices** to inspect the document. Recent estimates provide a shortcut to quotations you are still working on. For a printable breakdown, use [Reports](./reports.md) with an explicit date range.
