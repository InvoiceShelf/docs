# Dashboard

The dashboard summarizes the company currently selected in the application. Choose the company from the button at the top of the sidebar; **Administration** is a separate workspace for server management.

## Read the overview

The **Owed to you** panel separates overdue invoices, amounts due in the next 30 days and amounts due later. Customer, invoice and estimate counts appear below it. **View invoices** opens the invoice list.

![InvoiceShelf 3 dashboard with a populated demo company](/images/v3/dashboard.webp)

## Compare activity over time

The **Cash flow** chart shows Sales, Receipts, Expenses and Net income. Use the date-range control at the top right and the chart controls to change how you view the data. **Show as table** provides the figures without relying on the graph.

Invoices and receipts describe different events: creating an invoice does not itself record a payment. Check the [payments](./payments.md) and [expenses](./expenses.md) lists when reconciling a total.

## Follow up on outstanding work

Open an entry under **Due invoices** to inspect the document. Recent estimates provide a shortcut to quotations you are still working on. For a printable breakdown, use [Reports](./reports.md) with an explicit date range.
