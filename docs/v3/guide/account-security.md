# Your account and security

Open your account menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **User Settings**. These pages belong to your login; company preferences and administrator settings live elsewhere.

## Profile

Use **General** to maintain your name and email, and **Profile Photo** for your account image. Check the saved values before leaving. Changing your personal profile does not update the company address printed on invoices.

![InvoiceShelf 3 personal account settings](/images/v3/account.webp)

## Security and devices

Use **Security** for the available password and sign-in controls. **Devices** shows sessions you can review and revoke. Revoke a session when you no longer recognize or use that device; avoid keeping shared computers signed in.

## Connected apps

**Connected apps** lists integrations authorized by your login. Review the company and permissions of each connection, and revoke access that is no longer needed. An [AI assistant connection](./ai-assistants.md) is company-bound and remains subject to your role.

If a company page is missing, check [membership and roles](./members.md) rather than changing personal profile settings. Server-wide access is controlled separately by an administrator.
